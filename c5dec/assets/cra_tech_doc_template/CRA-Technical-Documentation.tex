\markboth{Introduction}{Introduction}

This document provides the technical documentation required by Article
31 and Annex VII of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (the Cyber Resilience Act)
for products with digital elements placed on the EU market.

\textbf{Product:} \textbf{?meta:product.name}\\
\textbf{Manufacturer:} \textbf{?meta:manufacturer.name}\\
\textbf{Document Version:} \textbf{?meta:version}\\
\textbf{Product Category:} \textbf{?meta:product.category}

\section{Purpose and Scope}\label{purpose-and-scope}

This technical documentation demonstrates compliance with the essential
cybersecurity requirements set out in Annex I of the CRA. It accompanies
the EU Declaration of Conformity and provides evidence that the product
meets the applicable requirements for its category.

\section{Document Structure}\label{document-structure}

This document follows the structure specified in CRA Annex VII:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Product Description} - General description and intended use
\item
  \textbf{Design and Development} - Architecture, components, and
  security design
\item
  \textbf{Cybersecurity Risk Assessment} - Identified risks and
  mitigation measures
\item
  \textbf{Applied Standards} - Harmonised standards and common
  specifications
\item
  \textbf{EU Declaration of Conformity} - Formal declaration per Annex V
\item
  \textbf{Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)} - Component inventory per
  Annex I Part II(1)
\end{enumerate}
